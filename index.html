Dunkin Donuts Menu With Prices

One of the biggest reasons people choose Dunkin' over premium coffee chains is simple: it costs less. But "less" still covers a wide range once you look across donuts, coffee, espresso drinks, and breakfast food. This guide to the Dunkin Donuts menu with prices breaks down what each category typically costs, so you can plan your order — and your budget — before you reach the counter. Treat every figure here as a typical range rather than a promise: prices vary by location, and they shift over time.

Dunkin' was founded in 1950 by William Rosenberg in Quincy, Massachusetts, and has spent 70-plus years perfecting the art of affordable indulgence. After dropping "Donuts" from its name in 2018 and joining Inspire Brands, the chain leaned even harder into beverages while keeping its bakery prices friendly. The Dunkin Donuts menu remains one of the best values in quick-service food and drink.

Donut Prices

The bakery case is where Dunkin' built its name, and it is still the cheapest way to treat yourself. A single classic donut — Glazed, Chocolate Glazed, Boston Kreme, Jelly-filled, Glazed Blueberry, Old Fashioned, or French Cruller — typically runs around $1.50 to $2. Fancier or filled varieties can nudge slightly higher. Buying in bulk is the smart move: a half dozen usually lands around $7 to $9, and a full dozen typically costs somewhere around $12 to $16, which brings the per-donut price down nicely for offices, parties, or weekend family runs. Munchkins donut holes are priced similarly per piece and are often sold in 10, 25, or 50-count boxes, making them one of the most economical shareable options on the menu. As always, prices vary by location.

Coffee and Espresso Drink Prices

Coffee is the core of the business, and pricing stays approachable. A medium hot Original Blend coffee typically costs around $2.50 to $3.50 — noticeably less than equivalent drinks at specialty coffee shops. Iced coffee runs a little higher, usually around $3 to $4 for a medium, with Cold Brew priced a step above that thanks to its longer steeping process. Espresso drinks climb from there: lattes, cappuccinos, and macchiatos generally fall around $3.50 to $5 depending on size, while specialty builds like the Shakin' Espresso or Dunkalatte can reach the upper end of that range. Matcha lattes sit in a similar band. Coolatta frozen drinks and Refreshers usually land around $3.50 to $5 as well. Adding flavor shots or cold foam typically adds a small upcharge.

Breakfast Food Prices

The all-day breakfast menu is priced for everyday mornings rather than special occasions. A Bacon, Egg & Cheese or Sausage, Egg & Cheese sandwich — on a croissant, bagel, or English muffin — typically costs around $4 to $6. Wake-Up Wraps, the smaller tortilla-wrapped option, usually come in a bit lower at roughly $2.50 to $3.50, making them a popular light-bite choice. Hash Browns generally run around $2 to $3, and bakery items like muffins and bagels typically sit around $2 to $3.50 each. Pairing a breakfast sandwich with a medium coffee often lands you a satisfying morning meal for well under $10 in most markets, though prices vary by location.

Why Prices Vary by Location

If you have ever noticed your usual order costing more on a road trip, you are not imagining it. Dunkin' locations are largely franchised, and individual operators set prices based on local rent, labor costs, and regional competition. Airport, downtown, and resort-area stores tend to charge premiums, while suburban locations are often cheaper. State and local taxes also nudge the final total. Seasonal and limited-time items — like the Fall 2026 Tiramisu Collection or pumpkin drinks — sometimes carry a small premium over core menu equivalents. The practical takeaway: use the ranges in this guide for planning, but check your local store's menu board or app for exact numbers.

Value Tips and Deals

Regulars rarely pay full sticker price. The Dunkin' rewards program earns points on every purchase toward free drinks and food, and members get periodic bonus offers. Ordering ahead in the app sometimes unlocks exclusive deals, and limited-time promotions — especially around new seasonal launches — can include discounted or bundled items. Buying donuts by the dozen instead of individually is the simplest instant saving.
